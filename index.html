Fruit Wonderland

Official Privacy Policy & Data Safety Disclosures

Last Updated: October 30, 2026
Summary for Players: Fruit Wonderland is a casual match-3 puzzle game. We respect your privacy. We do not ask for or collect your personal identifying details (like your name, home address, phone number, or payment card). We only process technical data (such as advertising IDs and crash analytics) through trusted partners like Google AdMob to deliver ads, reward videos, and keep the game running smoothly.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the developer of Fruit Wonderland ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and discloses information when you download, install, and play our Android mobile game Fruit Wonderland (Package Name: com.aistudio.fruitwonderland.qzpkm).

By installing or using Fruit Wonderland, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use our game.

2. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide Directly

Fruit Wonderland does not require you to create an account, register, or provide any personal information (such as your full name, email address, physical address, or phone number) to play.

If you contact us via email for support or feedback, we will receive your email address and any details you choose to include in your correspondence. We use this strictly to address your inquiry.

B. Information Collected Automatically

When you play Fruit Wonderland, certain non-personally identifiable technical information may be collected automatically by third-party SDKs integrated into the app (specifically Google AdMob and Google Play Services):

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID), device model, manufacturer, and screen resolution.
  • Operating System Data: Android OS version and system language.
  • Network & IP Information: Internet connection status, coarse geographic location (country/city level derived from IP address), and mobile carrier.
  • App Telemetry & Gameplay State: Game session duration, level progression, moves made, in-game achievements, and crash logs or ANR (Application Not Responding) diagnostic traces.

3. Device Permissions Used

Fruit Wonderland requests minimal, least-privilege permissions required for gameplay and advertising:

Permission Type Purpose
android.permission.INTERNET Normal (Install-time) Allows loading advertisements (rewarded ads for extra moves/lives) and checking for game updates.
android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Normal (Install-time) Allows the game to detect active Wi-Fi or mobile data connections before requesting ad content.
android.permission.VIBRATE Normal (Install-time) Enables tactile haptic feedback effects when matching fruits and popping boosters.

5. How We Use Collected Information

We and our advertising partners use the collected technical information for the following legitimate purposes:

  • To run, update, and improve the gameplay mechanics and performance of Fruit Wonderland.
  • To persist game progress, unlocked levels, high scores, and star ratings on your local device.
  • To serve contextual or personalized advertisements and reward video delivery in compliance with Google Play policy.
  • To detect, prevent, and fix technical glitches, crashes, and unauthorized exploitation.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families Policy)

Fruit Wonderland is designed as a fun, family-friendly casual puzzle game. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area).

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information to us without your consent, please contact us immediately at kunakom01@gmail.com. We will take prompt steps to remove such information from our records.

8. User Rights & Data Protection (GDPR / CCPA / CPRA)

A. European Economic Area (EEA) & UK Users (GDPR)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), European users have specific rights, including:

  • Right of Access & Rectification: You may request information about any personal data held about you.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You may request the deletion of any data you provided to our support team.
  • Consent Management: You can manage or withdraw consent for personalized advertising through your Android device settings.

B. California Residents (CCPA / CPRA)

California residents have the right under the CCPA/CPRA to:

  • Know what personal data categories are collected and shared.
  • Request deletion of collected personal information.
  • Opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal data for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not sell personal data for monetary consideration.

C. How to Opt Out of Personalized Ads on Android

You can reset or opt out of interest-based ads directly on your Android device at any time:

  1. Open your device Settings.
  2. Navigate to Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  3. Tap Delete advertising ID or Reset advertising ID.

9. Data Security & Storage

Your game progress (level scores, earned stars, and inventory) is stored securely on your local device storage. Technical data processed by our third-party advertising partners is transmitted using standard industry-grade HTTPS/TLS encryption.
